# AutoMindful 0.1.46

- In Training and Recovery, the first in-game hunger or thirst moodle can
  prompt a search for safe food or water. Nearby trips still respect the
  scouting settings.
- The pathfinder can consider alternate legal approach tiles for known food
  and water. If no approach works, AutoMindful can move on to another source.
- Low health no longer stops safe survival care. Death still ends the session.
  Manual actions and immediate danger take priority.
- If no usable food is confirmed, eligible Lifestyle Meditation can help
  with hunger even when a water route fails. It provides neither calories
  nor water.

Check **Activity** if care cannot continue. AutoMindful still needs safe
supplies and legal access to use them.
