# AutoMindful 0.1.20

- After two genuine path failures returning from a care trip, the final walk can aim for the survivor's saved ground position on the same original tile rather than its center. It does not choose a different tile, teleport, or relax any route or safety check.
- The three-attempt limit, normal timed actions, manual cancellation and other work taking priority are unchanged. No new automatic book reading or borrowing is caused by the retry.
- The client log identifies each failed attempt and the aim it used without logging absolute map coordinates.
