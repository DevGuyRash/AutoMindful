Second Nature (formerly AutoMindful) looks after your survivor: food, water, reading, rest, sleep, the toilet, washing and wounds. [b]Autopilot[/b] does it while you play or step away. With Lifestyle: Hobbies it also trains Meditation and Yoga for you, and at level 10 Autopilot keeps doing the looking after on its own. Lifestyle is optional; without it, right-click your survivor to start Autopilot.

[h2]Jobs[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Yoga[/b], [b]Meditation[/b] or [b]Yoga and Meditation[/b]: train toward level 10 with Lifestyle's own actions and XP, taking care breaks and resuming by itself.
[*][b]Reach a moodle tier[/b]: practise until a Mindfulness or Zen tier is active, then stop.
[*][b]Autopilot[/b]: look after the survivor without training. It works at any rank and is what a training job turns into at level 10.
[/list]

[h2]Autopilot[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Knows whether you are there.[/b] While you or another mod keep the action queue busy, a carried bite or sip goes between two actions; nothing is cancelled or reordered. While you stand around it does small things. Once you are away (3 minutes without input, or [i]I am going AFK[/i]) it does everything it can.
[*][b]Danger in degrees.[/b] Being chased, touched or on fire: hands off. A zombie in view, a wound or a parked car: it stays where it is but still eats, drinks and bandages.
[*][b]First aid.[/b] Bleeding and open wounds get the best dressing carried or within reach.
[*][b]Looks ahead.[/b] Shows how long known food and drink last in real time and what would go wrong first, warns you as you leave, and plays a sound only you hear when it cannot keep the survivor safe.
[*][b]Water on the survivor.[/b] Keeps carried bottles full; the game drinks from them by itself.
[*][b]Sleeps in a bed[/b] in the same building and walks back.
[*][b]Never quietly gives up.[/b] Only Stop or death ends it. Problems back off and retry, and it carries on after you load in again.
[*][b]Diet goal.[/b] Auto keeps a healthy weight; or prefer high protein, more or fewer calories, low carb or low fat when choices are close.
[*][b]Cooks.[/b] When only raw food is left, it cooks it on a powered stove or oven, a microwave (never anything metal), a propane grill, or, with no power, a fireplace, antique stove, charcoal grill or campfire that it fuels, lights and puts out again. Food comes from any cupboard, bag, the floor or a dead body's pockets indoors. Each dish comes out cooked, never burnt, and the heat goes off with the last one. With a pot and water it makes soups and stews. Raw food that would spoil soon cooks in the same batch, leftovers go into a powered fridge or freezer, and frozen food is taken out to thaw ahead of the next meal. Stoves and fires someone else is using are left alone.
[*][b]Cooking practice.[/b] When nobody is hungry it cooks spare raw food that would spoil for Cooking XP, and shows the time to the next level.
[*][b]Last resort:[/b] plain unsafe water after a minute of Dying of Thirst with nothing clean left. It never eats rotten or poisonous food.
[/list]
Each of these has a switch under Settings > Autopilot.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
Right-click the ground, open Lifestyle's [b]Wellness[/b] menu and choose [b]Second Nature[/b], or press [b]0[/b] for the window. While a job runs, right-click your survivor for Stop, Switch and I am going AFK.

The window has [b]Now[/b] (what it is doing and why), [b]Supplies[/b] (what it knows of nearby), [b]Kitchen[/b] (every stove, grill and fire nearby and what it needs, food, fridges and freezers), [b]Log[/b] (recent decisions and Copy report), [b]Guide[/b] and [b]Settings[/b]. Drag a corner to resize; double-click it to fit.

Under Supplies, choose how far care may reach: only what you carry, what is within arm's reach, or a short walk to nearby supplies (default 10 tiles). Borrowed items go back where they came from.

[h2]Logs[/h2]
Decisions, containers and the survivor's state go to [i]Zomboid/Lua/AutoMindful/log-N.txt[/i]. If something goes wrong, use Copy report in the Log tab and include it in your report.

[h2]Requirements and multiplayer[/h2]
Project Zomboid Build 42.22+. Yoga and Meditation also need [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3403870858]Lifestyle: Hobbies by Angry[/url] with Wellness enabled; Autopilot works without it. Tested on 42.22.2 with Lifestyle 0.4.0.3. No Lifestyle files are bundled.

All Second Nature code runs on the client. On a server it must still be in the server's mod list, and the server's rules and, with Lifestyle, its own requirements apply.
