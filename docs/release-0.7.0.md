# Second Nature 0.7.0

- **Its own Workshop item and mod ID:** Second Nature is now a new Workshop item with the mod ID `SecondNature`, and the old AutoMindful item has been removed. For a save that used AutoMindful, subscribe to Second Nature, then turn it on in that save's mods (Load, select the save, More, Mods) in place of the missing AutoMindful. Settings, logs and the survivor's progress carry over. Servers use `Mods=SecondNature` and the new item in `WorkshopItems=`.
- **Body tab:** wounds, nutrition and what is coming up, read live from the game. Each wound shows its conditions in colour, its next step and the item it uses, or what is missing nearby, and how soon it heals. Nutrition shows the weight, its trend and how long until the next weight line in game days and real hours, the calories that stop a loss or a gain, and carbs, fat and protein with their effects. A **Coming up** timeline puts every next event in one list.
- **One-click first aid:** **Treat** sees one wound through, **Treat all** every wound, and **Dressing** replaces a dressing with the clean one you choose, from the Body tab or the Health tab. It runs as a short First aid job, or inside a running Autopilot, and says what is left when something is missing.
- **Health tab:** a Second Nature button after Fitness, the weight and its trend in the bottom strip, and treat, dress and treat all on each body part's right-click menu.
- **Food tooltips:** hover any food for what eating it does to this survivor now: calories and stored energy, whether it starts or stops weight gain, the protein bonus to Strength XP, where its energy comes from, and how it suits the diet goal. **Nutrition on food** in Settings turns it down or off.
- **Moodle timers:** hovering Zen or Mindfulness shows how long until the tier drops and until it is gone, live, in game and real time, with a bar from green to dark red.
- **Fires:** fuel goes least valuable first (twigs and scrap before planks and logs), and books, money, clothes, keepsakes and favourites are never burned. Fires light with the cheapest starter, petrol when there is no tinder, or a notched plank and a stick when there is no starter.
- **New logo** with the Second Nature name.
- **Under the hood:** the nutrition forecasts run the game's own rules, now checked against the installed game, and the Zen countdown reads Lifestyle's own decay. The Auto diet goal uses the game's weight bands, so 75 and 85 kg count as the game counts them.
