# AutoMindful 0.3.0

Recovery can now be left in charge.

- **Knows whether you are there.** It helps between your queued actions, does small things while you stand around, and does everything once you are away (3 minutes without input, or **I am going AFK**). It no longer sits down or walks off two seconds after you stop moving.
- **Danger in degrees.** A zombie in view, a wound or a parked car used to switch all care off, even drinking from your own bottle. Now only being chased, touched or on fire does. This stands behind every training job too.
- **First aid.** Bleeding, open wounds and dirty bandages are dressed with the game's own action.
- **Looks ahead.** How long known food and drink last in real time, and what would go wrong first. Says so when you leave, and warns out loud when it cannot keep the survivor safe.
- **Water on the survivor.** Keeps carried bottles full; the game drinks from them by itself.
- **Sleeps in a bed** within range and walks back, after a bite and a sip.
- **Never quietly gives up.** Failed routes, interrupted actions and internal errors back off instead of ending the job. Escape steps back. It carries on after loading.
- **Stuck spots.** Several failed walks from one tile are recognized; it tries to step off and asks for help.
- **Last resort.** Plain unsafe water after a minute of Dying of Thirst with nothing clean left.

First aid, care between actions, bottles, beds, the last resort, the sound and carrying on after loading are switches under **Settings > Recovery**.

- **Window:** resizing follows the mouse exactly. Double-click the corner to fit the contents, or the bottom edge to fit the height.
