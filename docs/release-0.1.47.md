# AutoMindful 0.1.47

- A failed approach to known painkillers no longer interrupts practice again
  on the same route just because a short timer expired. AutoMindful checks
  other eligible sources and reconsiders the route when navigation changes.
- The console log now records what interrupted practice, why a supply route
  was skipped, what permits another attempt, and the tiles involved in a
  failed return to the training spot.
